# Launch gate

Run every check for every segment. Record the evidence beside each: a command and its output, a file path, a still. A failed check holds the segment until its fix lands.

## The feature is real for the viewer

1. **Live.** The feature is deployed to production and on for the audience the video targets. Evidence: the flag or capability state for that audience, and the deploy that carries it.
2. **Findable.** A new user reaches it from the main navigation, not only from settings or a deep link. Evidence: the nav entry in the source, or a still of it.
3. **Reachable on day one.** A user who signs up from the video can try it without waiting on a third party. When setup needs an outside step (a provider enabling an account, a government credential), the script says so in the flaw beat.
4. **Filmed on the release build.** The demo spec passes on the commit being released. Record that commit beside the footage.
5. **Clean footage.** No product bug from the friction log shows in any beat except the flaw. A bug that shows elsewhere holds the segment until its fix lands and the spec is re-filmed.

## The link goes somewhere

6. **Announced.** The changelog or release note for the feature is published, or ready to publish the same hour as the post.
7. **Landing.** "Link below" opens a page about this feature or straight into signup, with one tracked short link per channel.

## The claims hold

8. **Every number cites a source** the founder can open.
9. **No borrowed authority.** A government body, bank or partner is named only to say the product works with it, never implying endorsement. "Compliant" or "official" appear only with proof.
10. **Demo data only.** Every name, email, company and amount on screen is invented or the founder's own.

## Someone is there

11. **Owner.** One person answers comments and support for the first 48 hours.
12. **Alerts.** Errors on the feature's routes alert that person.
13. **Pull plan.** The founder knows whether a break means flag off, hotfix, or take the post down.
